# Data spec — Inventory & Fulfillment Network

**Source of truth:** [`data/numbers-bible.json`](../../data/numbers-bible.json). All entities
are fictional.

This spec governs the `warehouses`, `stock_levels`, and reorder-history seed generators
(E3#5), the inventory browser (E4#2), the reorder queue (E4#5), and the auto-reorder agent's
policy (E5#2). The seeded reorder history must look like the agent's own policy produced it,
so the agent's future decisions are continuous with history.

## 2. Stock snapshots

- `stock_levels` is a **weekly snapshot** time series per (SKU, FC): `on_hand`, `reserved`,
  `inbound` (open PO quantity), `reorder_point`, `days_of_cover`. Snapshots carry quarter
  tags relative to `DEMO_EPOCH` (architecture.md), `data_origin='seed'`.
- Demand per (SKU, FC) week is derived from the order generator's actual draws
  ([`orders.md`](orders.md)) — inventory is reconstructed *from* demand plus the PO history,
  not drawn independently. This is what makes the stockout → late-order causality literal.
- Velocity classes (per FC): **A** (top 15% of SKUs, ~70% of units), **B** (next 35%, ~25%),
  **C** (tail 50%, ~5%). Class determines service level and review cadence below.

## 3. Stock policy

| Parameter | A | B | C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Target service level (cycle) | 98% (z = 2.05) | 95% (z = 1.65) | 90% (z = 1.28) |
| Days-of-cover target | 21 | 30 | 45 |
| Review cadence | continuous | weekly | biweekly |

**Reorder point:** `ROP = d̄ × LT + z × σ_d × √LT`, where `d̄` = trailing-8-week mean daily
demand, `σ_d` its standard deviation, `LT` = supplier lead time in days
([`suppliers.md`](suppliers.md) §4: import 28–42 d, domestic 3–10 d).

**Order quantity (EOQ-lite):** `Q = max(d̄ × cover_target, MOQ)`, rounded to the supplier's
case pack. MOQs: import suppliers 200–500 units/SKU, domestic 50–150.

**Trigger:** reorder when `on_hand + inbound < ROP` — exactly the auto-reorder agent's policy
(E5#2), so seeded PO history and future agent-written POs (`data_origin='agent'`) are
indistinguishable in shape.

**Supplier selection with SLA penalty:** when a SKU is multi-sourced, choose supplier by
`score = landed_cost × (1 + λ × (1 − on_time_rate_trailing_4q))` with λ = 0.6. Through 2025
this still mostly picks Brightline on cost; by 2025-Q4 Brightline's decayed on-time (54%)
flips the score and volume visibly routes to Dragon Gate, then Saigon from 2026-Q1
(catalog.md §4) — the seeded history must show that flip in the PO record.

## 4. Seeded reorder history

- Historical POs are generated by *simulating* the §3 policy over the weekly demand series:
  when the simulated position crosses ROP, emit a PO (suppliers.md §4 shapes) with an
  `audit_log` row attributed `agent:auto-reorder` (reason string includes the ROP math), or
  `human:ops` for the ~15% of POs above the auto-approval spend threshold — matching the
  approval-queue behavior in E4#5/E5#2.
- **Auto-approval threshold:** POs under $25,000 landed auto-approve; above that, a seeded
  human approval row (1–48 h latency) precedes the PO issue.
- **Stockout windows:** when a PO docks late (Brightline/Apex per suppliers.md §2), the
  affected (SKU, FC) on-hand hits zero and stays there until dock; orders in that window
  backorder (orders.md §5). Expected texture: stockout-days per quarter for
  electronics/SKA SKUs roughly triple from 2023 to 2025, then fall back through 2026 —
  the inventory-side signature of the bible's on-time arc (96 → 88 → 92.8).
- Low-stock rows (`on_hand + inbound < ROP`) at the seed horizon: ~4% of A SKUs, ~7% of B,
  ~10% of C — enough to make the reorder queue and red-flag rows in the inventory browser
  (E4#2) non-empty on demo day.

## 5. Reconcile hooks

1. Exactly 11 warehouses; no stock/order row references an FC before its opening quarter.
2. Conservation per (SKU, FC, week): `on_hand(t+1) = on_hand(t) + receipts − shipments ±
   adjustments`, with adjustments (shrinkage) < 0.5% of units.
3. Simulated policy fidelity: ≥95% of seeded POs satisfy "position < ROP at issue time";
   PO quantities match the EOQ-lite formula within rounding/MOQ.
4. Stockout-days series for tariff-exposed categories peaks in 2025 and declines through
   2026, consistent with the bible's `on_time_delivery_pct` recovery.
5. Fulfillment cost per order, derived from FC throughput and the modeled cost schedule,
   tracks the bible's `fulfillment_cost_per_order_usd` column (4.9 → 7.6 → 6.1) within ±2%.
